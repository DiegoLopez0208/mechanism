# MULTI-PLATE CLUTCH

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[Ortlinghaus · clutch plates](https://www.ortlinghaus.com/english/products/plates/plates.html)

Supported principle / published information: Friction plate / steel counterplate pairing.

## Estimated details

Nine-plate stack, splines, dimensions and prescribed engagement. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.

## Shots

1. Alternating friction interfaces — Outer-tab friction plates — Inner-splined steel counterplates.
2. Compress the plate pack — Outer basket and inner hub — Engagement shown schematically.
3. Separate the friction stack — Basket, hub, plates and pressure ring — No torque or thermal calculation.
