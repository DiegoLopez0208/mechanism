# POPPET CHECK VALVE

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[Swagelok · check valves, p.2](https://www.swagelok.com/downloads/webcatalogs/en/ms-01-176.pdf)

Supported principle / published information: Spring / poppet / seat construction and opening principle.

## Estimated details

Dimensions, lift and spring; no pressure prediction. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.
