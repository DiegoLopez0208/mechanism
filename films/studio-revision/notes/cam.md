# ECCENTRIC CAM

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[CMU · cams and followers](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rapidproto/mechanisms/chpt6.html)

Supported principle / published information: Cam / follower / guide construction and motion conversion.

## Estimated details

Circular eccentric dimensions and spring geometry. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.

## Shots

1. A rotating eccentric — 18 mm cam radius · 4 mm offset — 5 mm roller radius.
2. Track the roller — The follower stays on its guide — Prescribed geometric contact.
3. Separate the follower — Roller, stem, guide and return spring — Spring forces are not simulated.

The original Studio compositor renders the meshes. Sources: native/catalog.js, native/geometry.js, native/models.js, native/models-fluid.js and native/director.js in this collection.
