# DISC BRAKE

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[Brembo · disc / caliper / pad construction](https://www.brembo.com/en/solutions/for-your-car/gt-bm6-kit?sc_lang=en)

Supported principle / published information: Disc, fixed multi-piston caliper and friction-pad arrangement.

## Estimated details

Four-piston teaching model, dimensions and movement; not a BM6 replica. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.

## Shots

1. Rotor and opposed pads — Fixed opposed-piston construction — Vented rotor.
2. Expose the pistons — Pistons push pads toward the rotor — Motion enlarged for visibility.
3. Separate the caliper — Pistons, pads, bridge and rotor — No braking-force simulation.

The original Studio compositor renders the meshes. Sources: native/catalog.js, native/geometry.js, native/models.js, native/models-fluid.js and native/director.js in this collection.
