# RACK & PINION

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[CMU / KHK · gear geometry](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rapidproto/mechanisms/chpt7.html)

Supported principle / published information: Module and involute pinion conventions; rack pitch πm.

## Estimated details

Rack length, rail and chosen 20-tooth pinion. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.
