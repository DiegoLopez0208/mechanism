# GENEVA INDEXER

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[CMU · Geneva wheel](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rapidproto/mechanisms/examples.html)

Supported principle / published information: Four-slot indexing principle and locking surfaces.

## Estimated details

35 mm centre distance; chosen thicknesses and clearances. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.

## Shots

1. Continuous to intermittent — Four radial slots — One input turn · 90° output index.
2. The pin enters a slot — Indexing alternates with dwell — Four-slot geometric construction.
3. Separate the driver — Drive pin, locking sector and wheel — No impact or load simulation.

The original Studio compositor renders the meshes. Sources: native/catalog.js, native/geometry.js, native/models.js, native/models-fluid.js and native/director.js in this collection.
