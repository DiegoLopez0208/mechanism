# TRAPEZOIDAL SCREW

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[Roton · trapezoidal lead screws](https://www.roton.com/products/trapezoidal-lead-screws-nuts/general-information/)

Supported principle / published information: Trapezoidal thread has 30° included angle.

## Estimated details

Selected 16 mm crest diameter, 4 mm lead, nut and clearances. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.

## Shots

1. Helical trapezoidal flanks — 30° included thread angle — Illustrative single-start 4 mm lead.
2. Open the travelling nut — Matching internal helical flanks — 4 mm travel per revolution.
3. Separate nut and screw — Continuous trapezoidal ridge — No friction or load calculation.
