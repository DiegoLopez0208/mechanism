# PLANETARY TRAIN

12-second native Studio construction study, 1600 × 900 at 30 fps. Silent.

## Reference

[KHK · internal gears / planetary conditions](https://khkgears.net/pdf/2023/internal-gears.pdf)

Supported principle / published information: Ring = sun + 2 × planet; equal-spacing assembly condition.

## Estimated details

Chosen 18/18/54 combination and carrier dimensions. This is an authored educational model, not manufacturer CAD. Motion is prescribed or ideal kinematics, not a load, friction, leakage, fatigue or thermal simulation.
