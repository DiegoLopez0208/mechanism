The gate

A spec that does not add up is not rendered.

The model is run against what is published. The Rubik cube is 26 pieces that really turn, with no tables: the 20-move superflip has to flip all 12 edges, the longest sequence has to come back after 1,260 repeats.

Change a tooth, a firing order, a claim: it says exactly what broke. Try it on the hub: the viewer changes one published figure and runs the gate in your browser.

The verdict is never a bare pass: VERIFIED only if something was compared, nothing is pending and at least one figure comes from a source independent of the inputs. If everything matched but no check is independent (the figures share their source with the inputs, a drawn size is compared with the figure it was drawn from, or the inputs cannot be traced) it says CONSISTENT: a wrong source would pass too. Numbers given by the person who asked are tagged USER and are never verified. Otherwise it says NOTHING CHECKED, INCOMPLETE or DOES NOT PASS, and it refuses the leftovers of a template (placeholder addresses, TODO, a number with no claim). Show me what is only a guess.

Every object is listed, with the gate's own numbers and a badge, in the register of verified objects.

And a model has to be recognisable: a stranger shown a label-free still must name it. The hub failed that test, so it now has spokes and a rim.

Complete, recognised

What two runs do not show.

    
Two runs are two data points, not a success rate. I wrote the skill, chose one of the two objects and started the agents. They were fresh subagents with no context, not fully independent sessions, and the GPU was shared during the first run.

      
After these runs the gate became stricter: VERIFIED now needs a figure from a source independent of the model's inputs. The Antikythera spec has none (its six checks share a source with their inputs), so today it says CONSISTENT; the floppy still says VERIFIED. The run notes keep what the gate said when it ran.

      
Everything the agents did is in the logs, including the failures. The toolkit problems they hit (a gear train that silently took one tooth count per axis, holes that could not be rectangular, a stranger test that only worked on this repository's own examples) were fixed afterwards, and the version each run used is written next to it. The plan is 20 objects; these are the first two.

It looks things up before it draws anything.
The skill's job is evidence. The engine and the sound are built to serve it.

  

    
Research
Spec sheets, teardowns, official photos, published test vectors. It opens the pages and the pictures.

    
Spec
Parts with real dimensions, the evidence behind each number, and how it moves.

    
Gate
Sources, cross-checks, centre distances, published ratios and test vectors. Wrong specs stop here.

    
Render
Camera, sound and panels are directed from the spec: a film, a viewer and a 3D model.

spec
Published by the maker or a datasheet.

    
measured
Measured on an official photo, scale from a published size, error stated.

    
derived
A formula plus a cross-check against another published number.

    
estimated
Nobody publishes it. Drawn dashed, and said out loud.
