  • MeasuredmeasuredLens glass Ø18.2 / Ø16.4 mm (POCO), three lenses of Ø16 mm in a 20 × 56.5 mm pill (S26 Ultra), corner radius 11 mm vs 7 mm.
  • InsidemeasuredPOCO battery bay 65 × 97.5 mm, board 67 × 41 mm, SoC thermal pad 17.7 × 19.4 mm, from the maker's own spare-parts photos. Implied cell density ~1093 Wh/L.
  • DerivedderivedPixel pitch and diagonal give the published ppi; sensor sizes from megapixels × pixel size agree with the optical format.
  • DisplaysLTPS AMOLED with 3840 Hz PWM against LTPO AMOLED 2X with Privacy Display: each one has its own layer stack.
  • HonestestimatedThe S26 Ultra interior has no public photo, so it is drawn dashed and says so.

Four kinds of number. Never mixed.

If nobody publishes it, it is dashed. If it was measured, the photo and the error are named.

spec

Published by the maker or a spec sheet, and reviewed hands-on.

measured

Measured on an official photo, with the scale from the published body size.

derived

A formula plus a cross-check against another published number.

estimated

Nobody publishes it. Drawn dashed, and said out loud.
