# Examples to copy from

Part of the mechanism skill (SKILL.md). Pick the example closest to the object before writing `spec.js` (step 3).

`examples/lock` (a pin tumbler lock with a KW1 key: the published table must close and a wrong key must block the plug), `examples/winrar` (WinRAR unpacking, from RFC 1951), `examples/react` (the real React DOM recorded in jsdom; a model written from the reconciliation algorithm must predict which list items move), `examples/switch` (a Cherry MX2A Red key switch: the three published force points cannot come from one linear spring, and it says so), `examples/enigma` (the Enigma I rotor stack: wiring, notches and stepping are checked against the published tests AAAAA > BDZGO, rings BBB > EWTYX and the ADU > ADV > AEW > BFX double step; type on your keyboard in the viewer), `examples/k20a` (Honda K20A: a crank-slider engine with 16 valves; the displacement is computed from bore and stroke and the 1-3-4-2 order is checked against the crank), `examples/rubik` (the cube: the 20-move superflip must flip the 12 edges, the longest period must be 1,260, the positions 43,252,003,274,489,856,000), `examples/rar5` (software: the 87 bytes of a RAR5 archive WinRAR 7.23 writes, rebuilt byte for byte by a model written from the published format and accepted by the real UnRAR), `examples/mos6502` (the 6502 in its DIP-40: pins counted on the pinout against the published bus widths, leads measured against the DIP standard), `examples/rtx4090` (GeForce RTX 4090: TFLOPS, fillrates, bandwidth and memory capacity are computed from the published cores, clocks, bus and data rate and must match; the inside of the card is estimated), `examples/hub` (a three-speed planetary hub, fully declarative: the tooth counts are DERIVED from a forum post and cross-checked against the published 75 / 100 / 133 %, the sizes are ESTIMATED and dashed), `examples/phones` (POCO X8 Pro Max vs Galaxy S26 Ultra, measured on official photos), `examples/rpi4` (Raspberry Pi 4B from the official datasheet drawing), `examples/backpressure` (the first, a 2D machine for an idea).
