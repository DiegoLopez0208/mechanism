# Code of conduct

Be direct about the work and kind to the person. Disagree with a number, a source or a design as hard as the evidence allows; do not make it about who wrote it.

- Say what you checked and how. "I opened the datasheet and page 12 says otherwise" beats "this is wrong".
- Report a problem with the project, not with a person. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not welcome in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Credit sources and other people's work. THIRD_PARTY.md lists what is used and under which licence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads and block accounts that break these rules. To report a conduct problem privately, use GitHub's "Report" option on the content or a private security advisory on this repository.
