# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 0.10.0 - 2026-10-20

Studio looks like a product photograph.

- **Lighting:** the metal now reflects a product studio (an overhead key softbox, a broad front fill, side strips, a back rim panel; HDR) instead of a grey room, so machined parts read as metal with clean panel highlights.
- **Materials with surface detail:** procedural micro-detail in the shader (no textures, no UVs, deterministic, fixed to each part): brushed streaks on machined metal, mottling on cast metal, grain on plastics, rubber and boards; it fades out before it could shimmer. Painted parts have a clear coat.
- **Framing that shows the object:** a default view that looks along a flat object turns to the angle that shows the most of it, mode views that would show it small are replaced, and each tour stop ends with a slow push-in.
- Every Studio film, GIF and poster re-rendered; 720p copies on the `media` release. Golden frames: only the mode and exploded frames changed (reviewed), on Windows and Linux.

## 0.9.0 - 2026-10-20

Drawings you can trust as far as they were checked: Mechanism Draft (dimensioned SVG drawings of flat parts) and a gate that no longer calls agreement with one source "verified". Draft plugin 0.4.0.

- **README rewritten** around what the project is now: every number names its class (SPEC, USER, MEASURED, DERIVED, ESTIMATED), the verdict says how far it was really checked, Draft is a section of its own with the Raspberry Pi 4 drawing, and every example shows the gate's verdict today. The engine, encoder, style, length and golden-frame details moved to `docs/rendering.md`.
- **Every film re-rendered** with the current gate and director (receipts now say CONSISTENT where no check is independent; the exploded view holds still): the eleven examples in their normal and short cuts, the phones, and the Raspberry Pi 4 board as a Paper sheet; new GIFs, posters and 720p copies on the `media` release.
- Versions: Mechanism 0.9.0; the Draft plugin goes from 0.1.0 to 0.4.0, its last stage (0.2 geometry, 0.3 SVG export, 0.4 the first real part, and the BH-1 hardening below).

- Beta hardening BH-1 (branch `feature/draft-beta-hardening-05`), from the first stranger test of Draft 0.4. **The gate no longer calls a spec VERIFIED without an independent check.** A drawn dimension compared with the figure it was drawn from (`params`) is now classified as a transcription and never counts as independent; checks whose inputs cite no claim, and test vectors with no claim, do not establish independence either. VERIFIED needs at least one check whose sources are disjoint from its inputs'; everything else that passes is CONSISTENT, and the gate says why. Before, a spec whose only source was the user's request passed as VERIFIED. Four shipped examples change from VERIFIED to CONSISTENT, with the cause written in `docs/draft-evidence.md`: switch (its travel is compared with the claim it was drawn from), k20a and rubik (inputs not traced, figures on one page), enigma (test vectors cite no claim); the six with independent checks stay VERIFIED; no claim or source was added to keep a verdict. New evidence class `user` (DECLARED BY USER): a source of `kind: 'user'` with no url, claims and dimensions of `conf: 'user'`, drawn and counted as "given by the person who asked", never as compared or verified; a document with no url is refused with a pointer to `kind: 'user'`. Placeholder addresses now include every subdomain of `example.com/.org/.net` and any `.invalid`, `.test` or `.example` host. On a 2D drawing a SPEC or USER dimension must be a number its claim states (else it is DERIVED), a dimension's class must match its claim's, and a MEASURED one needs `method` and `uncertainty` (never turned into a tolerance); older 3D parts get warnings. SVG and Paper tell printed, derived (blue / teal dashed), measured (violet / ink dotted), user-declared and estimated dimensions apart (`data-evidence`, `dimCount.published/derived/measured/user`). The 1:1 SVG legend wraps or widens the document instead of being cut (a 21 mm part lost "NOT A MANUFACTURING DRAWING"); the part does not move. A composite callout ("20 x 12") carries `data-keys` and `data-values`. The gate's `dimensions:` line names each class (`checked against a document`, `derived`, `measured (with uncertainty)`, `declared by the user`, `estimated`) and ends `0 independently verified`; it no longer calls a user's numbers "compared with their source", and `export-svg.mjs` prints the same classes. The skill's description now names dimensioned SVG drawings of flat parts, and SKILL.md carries a minimal spec (a user source, `src` arrays, `params`) that a test runs through the gate; README, plugin and marketplace texts describe Draft as it is, with its development branches. Contract: `docs/draft-evidence.md`.

- Draft 0.4, the first real part (branch `feature/draft-real-rpi4-04`): `examples/rpi4-board`, the Raspberry Pi 4 Model B board outline and its four mounting holes from Raspberry Pi's mechanical drawing (PDF RP-008343-DS-1, the same file as RP-001185-MD-1) and DXF (RP-008342-DS-1), opened 2026-10-19 and cited, not redistributed: 85 x 56 mm, corner radius 3.0, holes Ø2.7 whose centres are 3.5 mm from the left, bottom and top edges, 58 x 49 mm apart (61.5 and 52.5 from the datums are DERIVED). Every drawn dimension is compared with the PDF; the pitch from the DXF coordinates is compared with the PDF's 58 and 49. Both files are the maker's (the DXF `derives` from the PDF), so the gate says CONSISTENT, not VERIFIED. A reference drawing only: the size is "approximately 85 mm x 56 mm" per Raspberry Pi and no tolerance is published; the 6 mm copper pads, connectors, components, heights and board thickness are omitted. Paper draws it, the SVG export writes it at 1:1 and fitted, Draft ships the spec. One layout fix: a linear dimension whose span is too short for its text now places the text just outside an end, with the dimension line run on to it (the 3.5 mm dimensions at 1:1 needed it; the demonstration SVGs are byte-identical). One wording fix in the gate: its dimensions line said "N part(s) mix sourced and estimated dimensions" for any part whose dimensions are weaker than its headline class, even when none is estimated (here SPEC and DERIVED); it now names the classes ("15 compared with their source (11 spec, 4 derived), 0 estimated, 1 part(s) mix confidence classes (spec, derived)"). The verdict, `verdict.mixed` and every rule are unchanged.

- Draft 0.3, engineering SVG export (branch `feature/draft-svg-03`): `node scripts/export-svg.mjs <spec.js> --mode geometry-1to1|sheet-fit --out <dir>` writes one SVG per 2D `drawing` part, with no browser. `kit/svg2d.js` writes the view set `geom2d` makes from `build()` (each contour now carries its id): circles as `<circle>`, rounded corners as exact arcs, the material as one even-odd path, sections hatched with a pattern, centre and hidden lines, dimensions whose values are the geometry's (`data-key`, `data-value`), coloured by evidence. `geometry-1to1` is 1 user unit = 1 mm of the part; `sheet-fit` is an A4 sheet with a title block and the scale written as not 1:1; text and strokes are document sizes in both. Labels avoid contours, dimension lines, other labels and the sheet border; a layout that cannot be legible stops the export and names the labels. Spec text is escaped, ids are slugs, the same spec gives the same bytes, a spec that fails the gate exports nothing, and files never replace one the tool did not write. Every file says what is known (valid geometry, dimensions compared / declared / estimated, the gate's verdict, the mesh check NOT RUN) and that it is not a manufacturing drawing. Draft ships the exporter. See `docs/draft-svg.md`.

- Draft 0.2, parametric vector geometry (branch `feature/draft-parametric-02`): a part of `kind: 'drawing'` declares a flat piece as data (`p`: an outline rect, circle or polygon in mm; holes with stable ids; polar and linear patterns; centre lines and arcs; an optional hatched section) and `paperScene: { kind: 'geometry' }` draws it with no 3D model and no drawing code. `kit/geom2d.js` is the source of truth: the gate (also in Node) checks every contour is closed and simple, every hole lies inside the material and none overlaps, computes exact areas and named dimensions (`outline.w`, `<hole>.d`, `<hole>.x`, `<pattern>.pcd`, ...) that `params` can compare with sources; the same module gives Paper a view set (exact arcs, hatch regions, dimension lines placed from the geometry) and one generic piece sheet draws it. A valid drawing is internal consistency, not evidence: the demonstration parts (`examples/demo-plate`, `demo-flange`, `demo-section`, synthetic and ESTIMATED) are NOTHING CHECKED, and the sheet counts the dimensions declared but not verified. The mesh area check stays NOT RUN on this path; Studio refuses a flat part. Draft carries the module and the three examples. See `docs/draft-geometry.md`.

- Studio camera, exploded view to the first tour stop: in the normal and extended cuts the whole exploded object is now held still for `director.explodedHold` seconds (0.9 by default) before the camera moves, and the move to the first stop is a 0.7 s smoothstep instead of the damped spring of f4495eb, which covered most of the way in two or three frames. The rest of the object fades as the camera starts (the fade no longer jumps to 70 % in one frame), the part card fades in as the camera lands, and the stop's bell still rings on the landing. Everything after the hold moves with it (normal and extended films are 0.5-1 s longer); the short cut keeps its timing and its audio is unchanged. `tests/camera.test.mjs` measures the hold, the move, the landing and the sync on lock, hub and rtx4090; the "exploded" golden frames (Windows and Linux) now show the exploded object.

- Ecosystem foundation (branch `ecosystem-foundation`): an engine dependency manifest (`kit/manifest.js`), Workflow and vector Paper without three.js, Paper's mesh adapter split from its vector engine, explicit NOT RUN reporting of checks that do not run, a Paper visual-contract test, and Mechanism Draft 0.1 as a standalone plugin in `plugins/mechanism-draft/`. Hardening: `build-skill.mjs` refuses dangerous `--out` folders and removes only files its previous build wrote; `new.mjs` escapes `--title` and `--fig`; `skill-doctor.mjs --strict` and distinct states for optional packs; CI runs the manifest, Paper, preflight, Draft and version tests; `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` now carries the repository version (0.8.1) under the version policy in CONTRIBUTING.md.

- Replace the first presentation prototypes with independent engines: Workflow is a Canvas2D industrial process scene; Paper is pure vector technical drawing with front/top/side orthographic views. Neither engine instantiates WebGL or composites Studio frames. Reuse evidence/model data, not the original scene rendering. Tests prohibit WebGL creation in both 2D engines and require byte-identical repeated CPU frames. Studio retains the product renderer.

- Presentations: name the normal director Studio; add Workflow (model-driven process stations) and Paper (real mesh contours/silhouettes, numbered details and evidence annotations). Four independent backgrounds: Graphite, Blueprint, Porcelain and Ivory. CLI, URL and spec selection; 1.5x 3D supersampling for opted-in presentations; crisp Workflow/Paper overlays and synchronized deterministic timelines/audio. Existing films remain unchanged without opt-in. Add configuration, layout and browser tests; run them in CI.

- Repeatability and regression: seed the GTAO denoiser's startup texture (the pass does not rotate noise between frames), preserve its sample/filter settings, and test bounded residual raster rounding. Four compact lossless golden frames per example on Windows/Linux, explicit regeneration, CI comparisons, and deliberately broken text/decor controls. No vendor rebuild or new dependency.

- Render: accurate full-range BT.601 JPEG to limited-range BT.709 conversion, explicit primaries/transfer tags, and an opt-in software WebCodecs H.264 path (`--encoder browser`) with capability fallback and a scene-dependent bitrate budget. Audio, clipping, exports, stills and remux remain supported. `validate-render.mjs` compares decoded Chromium pixels against lossless references, including every evidence panel. Published media is not replaced.

- Gate disclosures: displayed calculations without published comparators, tooth counts outside meshing links, and unknown spec/part keys now warn. Part cards and the register distinguish confidence from size comparisons, including which dimensions remain unchecked. These warnings do not change VERIFIED/CONSISTENT or count unchecked calculations as reproduced figures.
- Browser lifecycle: shared port-zero CDP sessions, private profiles, bounded operations/frame watchdogs, one retry for watchdog failures, and cleanup of the owned PID tree on failure or interruption. All five browser scripts use the helper.

## 0.8.1 - 2026-10-16

The gate stops agreeing with itself, and the site gets a face.

- **Gate: provenance.** Every published-figure check is now classified as independent (the source of the published figure differs from the sources of the inputs), same source, or with no sourced inputs, and the verdict says so (`provenance:` line, a warning). **VERIFIED now needs at least one independent check; when none is, the state is the new CONSISTENT** (the film's stamp, the badges and the register follow). A source can say it derives from another (`derives: [ids]`) and then counts as the same. Measured on the 11 examples and the two agent runs: the lock becomes CONSISTENT (all its figures come from one page, Lockwiki) and so would the Antikythera run (its six checks share a source with their inputs); the floppy, the RTX 4090, the hub, rar5, react and the rest keep VERIFIED.
- **Gate: captions.** A quantity typed in a part card or in the lead text of the film (a decimal or a number with a unit) must be traceable to a claim, a dimension, a position or a published or computed value (mm, in and cm conversions and 0.3 % rounding allowed), or the gate warns. Bare integers are ignored. On the examples it finds two cases, both typed derived values: the hub's sprocket pitch radius (36.5 mm, the formula gives 36.57) and WinRAR's 41 %.
- **Site: a mark of our own and a splash screen.** The mark is a gear drawn solid on the left and dotted on the right (what is published is solid, what is estimated is dotted and said so); it replaces the gear emoji as the favicon and the symbol in the nav, there is a 1200 x 630 card for shared links, and a splash that plays once per session (opacity and transform only, because the main thread is busy for about 3 s starting the 3D hero; it waits for the page to load, can be skipped with a click or a key, and is skipped for deep links, `?nosplash` and reduced motion).
- **Site: nav.** The link is "Runs", labels no longer wrap, and between 761 and 1120 px the look switches are no longer cut off (already broken on the published page).
- **Site: video URLs carry a version** so a browser does not keep an old copy of an asset that was replaced under the same name.
- **Films:** the lock, the hub and WinRAR were re-rendered: the lock's stamp now says CONSISTENT, the hub's sprocket card says 36.6 mm (the formula gives 36.57, the card said 36.5) and WinRAR's 41 % is in a claim.
- **Benchmark notes:** a dated addendum with what today's gate says about each agent run, without rewriting what was published before.

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-16

The first two unassisted agent runs, a visual preflight, and the toolkit problems those runs found.

- **Agent runs** (`docs/benchmark/`): the 3.5-inch floppy disk and the Antikythera mechanism, each modelled by a fresh agent with only the skill. The criteria were fixed before the results; the logs keep every failure. Antikythera passes the gate but was not recognised by a blind judge; the floppy was. One run is one data point.
- **Visual preflight** (`scripts/preflight.mjs`): steps through the whole timeline without rendering an MP4 and reports a camera inside a part, parts or the object out of frame, texts that overlap or run past the edge, and flat parts whose mesh does not cover its outline. A diagnostic: it exits 0 unless `--strict`.
- **Gate:** a dimension whose unit differs from its claim's (10 cm against a claim of 10 mm) now fails before rendering; two gears of different size on one body is an error with the advice (one body per gear plus `same` links), where it used to take the first gear's teeth silently.
- **Toolkit:** `plate` takes rectangular holes and `profile` takes polygon holes; `stranger.mjs` works on any page and saves the still under a neutral name; `new.mjs` accepts a folder that already holds a log; `render.mjs` prints the film length and the wall time apart.
- **Films:** long texts now fit the frame in the panels and the intro card (30 preflight warnings on the normal cuts became 12, none of them a failure); the receipt of the lock fits its ten rows; the K20A shots no longer spill past both sides; the RTX 4090 has its rear fan past the end of a shorter board (cited to a review) and 60 parts instead of 66. The affected films were re-rendered.
- **Skill:** sources that are scans, the preflight step, one tooth count per body, renders in the background, and a note that anything on a part that fades needs the same `cutaway`.
- **Site:** a section for the agent runs, video URLs with the release version so a browser does not keep an old copy, and the lists brought up to date.

## 0.6.0 - 2026-10-13

The pre-launch release: audited, faster to render, lighter to install.

- **Audit.** Two read-only audits (secrets, personal data and licences across the tree and all 34 revisions; a code-level security review). No secrets, no personal data, no local paths. Fixed what they found: `SECURITY.md` and `SKILL.md` now say plainly that the gate is not a sandbox (a spec is code: read it before running it, or use `node --permission` or a container); `registry.mjs` only accepts well-formed object names and escapes every id; the CI token is read-only; `.gitignore` covers render leftovers; the checksum of the vendored three bundle is in `THIRD_PARTY.md`.
- **Faster renders.** Headless Edge was drawing on the integrated GPU. `render.mjs`, `probe.mjs` and `stranger.mjs` now ask for the high-performance GPU: a blurred frame went from 289 to 86 ms, a 30 s film from 155 s to 92 s on the test machine (a laptop with an integrated and a discrete GPU).
- **Lighter install.** A partial sparse clone is 4 MB instead of 85 MB or more (the repository keeps the films and the site; the skill does not need them). The command is in the README.
- **Refined from fresh-session tests.** Three sessions with no context installed the skill and modelled a Lenovo Legion 5 and a Game Boy. They found: the skill told agents to write inside its own folder (not allowed under `.claude/skills/`): now `new.mjs --dir <work>` and a SKILL.md that works in the user's project; unit conversions counted as evidence: the verdict now says ONLY CONVERSIONS and does not count them; `sqrt` and friends work in calc expressions; and an agent ran `taskkill /IM node.exe`, so SKILL.md now forbids stopping processes by name. SKILL.md starts with an eight-step quick start and ends with a final-report format.
- **Linux.** Tried from scratch on Ubuntu 24.04 (WSL2, no Node, ffmpeg or Chrome installed beforehand, normal user): the README install, the 33 tests, the gates of all ten objects, stills and the film lint pass. Rendering a film without a GPU takes about 7 s per frame, so the README says to render where there is one.
- **Compound gears.** A pinion and a wheel on one shaft (every watch train, every gearbox) no longer break the tooth-phase solver: each mesh component is solved from its own root and checked as strictly as before. A fourth fresh session modelling the ETA 2824-2 movement found it and patched the kit locally; the same spec now passes with the stock kit. Speed-bar labels are per drive (`drive.outLabel`) and show decimals below 10 rpm.
- **Film lint.** `scripts/lint-film.mjs` renders one draft frame per phase and tour stop and reports EMPTY / LOOK / SMALL before the render. It found a real defect in the hub film (the planet gear stop aimed at an empty spot): fixed and re-rendered.
- Social preview image (`docs/social-preview.png`).
- 30 tests, now including the lock and a tampered key pin.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-13

- **Gate.** Rejects placeholder sources and claims (example.com, TODO), non-positive or non-finite dimensions, specs with no parts, calculated variables with no claim, expressions that give undefined / NaN / Infinity, invalid tolerances and ESTIMATED parts with no reason. New drafts from new.mjs fail until completed.
- **Verdict.** VERIFIED / INCOMPLETE / NOTHING CHECKED / DOES NOT PASS instead of a bare pass; the film receipt follows it. check-spec.mjs --json writes a reproducible report with spec and kit hashes.
- **Tests and CI.** tests/gate.test.mjs (17 cases, including a tampered RAR5 byte) and a GitHub Actions workflow that also checks that an unfilled template fails.
- **Recognisable.** spec.recognize (object, iconic features tied to parts, recorded blind test), ?bare stills and scripts/stranger.mjs. The hub and the first RAR5 film were not recognised and say so.
- **Software.** RAR5 (87 bytes rebuilt byte for byte, accepted by UnRAR) and WinRAR unpacking (DEFLATE decoded from RFC 1951 one token at a time against real WinRAR output, zlib and the stored CRC-32).
- Switch: the spring is ESTIMATED, and the force-curve note no longer implies the maker contradicts itself.
- Wording: "build inspectable models from evidence" instead of "exactly".
